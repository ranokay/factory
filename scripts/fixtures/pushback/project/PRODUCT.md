# Reading Tracker

A command-line reading tracker for one person who wants a weekly count without a spreadsheet.

## Who it's for

The single reader who keeps a plain-text reading log on their own machine.

## Goals

- Report the pages read in a week from `reading report`.
- Keep every output usable in a pipe.

## Non-goals

- No web dashboard or browser UI.
- No cloud sync or accounts.

## Quality bars

- **Platforms:** macOS and Linux.
- **Performance:** `reading report` returns in under 100 ms on a laptop.
- **Privacy:** the reading log never leaves the machine.
